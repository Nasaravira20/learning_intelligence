# Teaching review checklist

A lesson can be shown to the learner who started it. It stays `pending_human_review` until a person checks factuality and teaching quality. This phase does not award mastery.

Review the saved lesson for one objective, not the whole topic.

## Factuality

- The objective code on the lesson is the current plan step.
- The explanation, example, and worked steps describe that objective and do not add a citation, DOI, quotation, or effect size.
- The hidden answer is not shown in the first hint.
- Nothing in the activity text claims the learner has mastered the objective.

## Teaching quality

- The activity list includes the primary strategy and each supporting strategy from the saved selection.
- A check for understanding appears before the last activity.
- A request for more support inserts another high-support activity instead of repeating the same step unchanged.
- A successful step lowers support on the next activity by one level.

## After review

- Record the reviewer, the lesson id, and whether the lesson is factually acceptable.
- Do not mark the lesson reviewed by editing the database check. That status stays pending until a later phase stores the review.
- Do not start evaluation or mastery from this checklist.
